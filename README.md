## 🖥️ About the Project
For the personal project proposal, I would like to create a website called StudySpace. This website could potentially aid students (whether they’re in college or in highschool) with studying for their courses, looking at upcoming work (like upcoming due dates for assignments or exams, or upcoming goals that they would like to reach). Many students struggle with balancing both their academic time and personal time, and keeping their academic resources in one accessible place. They often either forget to study, forget certain resources 
they would’ve wanted to study with, or what they need is on different websites/apps. Personally, when it comes to studying, I find myself very unorganized, and I feel the need to have two planners just to plan my way around how my academic week is going to look like.
With the way I would like my website StudySpace to look like, I would want my users to be able to login (or sign up if they are a new user). I will try to implement this by either using PHP (for temporary user data storage) or SQL (to store user data in a database, so that even after the website is closed, their data will not be forgotten).After they login, they will be referred to a dashboard, which will have their data portrayed onto it. 

It will have data like upcoming tasks, their courses, a calendar (for them to know what day of the week it is), and completed tasks (which will be in a drop down format, so that they can hide the completed tasks if they do not want to see them). Completed tasks will look different then upcoming tasks. I might have it so that a checkmark appears once they click on the circle to mark the task as complete, or I might have it so that there is a strike mark appearing over the text to signify that it is completed (likely using the 's' tag) They will also have a button on the bottom (and in the navigation bar) which will be colored differently from the theme and titled “Let’s Study”. When this button is clicked, it will redirect them to another HTML file that will have a stopwatch feature in it, with the buttons start/stop. This HTML file will have a very relaxing and visual appeal to it, but it is not meant to be distracting, since the users would likely want to stay focused on what they are studying for.
My website StudySpace will have a navigation bar, which will allow users to access the Dashboard, Let’s Study feature, weekly overview, and resources. This navigation bar will be located on the side (left) of the page, and will be hidden unless the user clicks/interacts with the button. The dashboard will redirect them to the dashboard, and the Let’s Study feature will redirect them to the Let’s Study stop watch. 


Their weekly overview will redirect them to another page where they will be able to see their data throughout the whole week. Before they study, they will be asked what class they are studying for. This way, when it comes to the weekly overview page, they will be allowed to see data for all of their classes (not just a big chunk of data). They’ll see the courses separated by boxes, and they’ll be able to see what tasks they completed for that certain course, along with how many hours they spent studying for that course. And at the very top, I plan to add all of the time that they spent studying for each course and displaying their weekly study time (time combined from all classes for that week).
When they would like to access their resources, this will be implemented in the form of links, pdfs uploaded into the website, and even images (of their homework, or screenshots of websites that they found helpful for studying). If users want to change their theme, I might try to implement a settings section (that will be available when they click on their username under the navigation bar) that will allow them to change the theme (likely titled Appearance) an About section (where they can get a guide for helpful information to guide them through the app) and a Privacy and Security Section (where they will be allowed to edit/see info like their birthday, name, and other things about them. I would want to have a section where they could edit/see their password, but for the sake of security I feel like that would be difficult to implement, since most websites have sent you emails with instructions on how to reset your password.


I believe with this website, several students (including me) could use it to track exam deadlines, how much they’ve studied for each course (and each exam) and allow them to further improve the system/way that they use to study for their assignments and exams. This is important to explore since it can help students focus and study for their exams. I’ve done my research across both microsoft edge and google, none of these platforms have a free and accessible website that allows users to track their progress and have their own personal space. When I was able to find resources, they were either applications that a person has to download on their computer, or a website that a user has to pay for in order to use.
This project addresses a common problem in web applications. The need for a simple, accessible, and user-friendly interface for students to use for their everyday/and study tasks. And it can be hosted for them to use for free on their own time! Rather than having to either pay to use a website, or having to use several different websites and having a cluttered space, students can just use StudySpace and have everything that they need organized.
